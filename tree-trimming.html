Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most — the same ones people ask Siri, Alexa, and Google.

How much does tree trimming cost?

Tree trimming is priced by the tree — its size, species, condition, and how much growth needs to come off — so Strong Roots Co. doesn't use flat rates. Estimates are always free. Call (801) 330-1444, and we'll visit your property in West Jordan, the Salt Lake Valley, or Park City and quote the exact price first.

When is the best time of year to trim trees in Utah?

For many Utah trees, late fall and winter are ideal because trees are dormant and their branch structure is easy to see. That said, dead, damaged, or hazardous limbs should come off right away, any season. Strong Roots Co. trims year-round across the Salt Lake Valley and Park City — call (801) 330-1444 to ask about your trees.

Will trimming my trees help them survive storms?

It helps. Removing dead wood, weak limbs, and overcrowded growth reduces the wind and snow load a tree carries, which lowers the chance of limbs breaking in canyon winds or heavy Utah snowstorms. Strong Roots Co. prunes for structure and balance with every trim. Call (801) 330-1444 for a free estimate before storm season.

How often should I have my trees trimmed?

Most shade and ornamental trees do well with a trim every few years, while fast growers and fruit trees may need attention more often. The right timing depends on the species, age, and health of each tree. During a free estimate, Strong Roots Co. can look at your trees and recommend a schedule. Call (801) 330-1444.

Do you trim trees in Park City?

Yes. Strong Roots Co. trims and prunes trees in Park City and Summit County, including aspen care, pine pruning, and defensible-space thinning around mountain homes. We're based in West Jordan and also serve the Salt Lake Valley and Utah, Davis, and Weber counties. Estimates are free — call (801) 330-1444 to book yours.
