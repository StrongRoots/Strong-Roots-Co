Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most — the same ones people ask Siri, Alexa, and Google.

How much does tree removal cost in West Jordan?

Tree removal cost depends on the tree's size, location, and how close it is to your home, fences, or power lines, so Strong Roots Co. doesn't charge a flat rate. Instead, estimates are always free. Call (801) 330-1444 and we'll visit your West Jordan or Salt Lake Valley property and give you an exact price before any work begins.

Can you remove a tree that's leaning over my house?

Yes. Leaning and hazardous trees are some of the most common removals we handle. Strong Roots Co. takes trees down in small, controlled sections, working carefully around roofs, fences, and landscaping. Call (801) 330-1444 for a free estimate, and if the tree looks unstable, treat it as an emergency — we're available twenty-four hours a day.

Do you clean up after removing a tree?

Yes — every Strong Roots Co. job finishes with a full cleanup. We haul off the trunk sections and brush from the removal and tidy the work area before we leave, so your yard is ready to use again. If you'd like the stump gone too, ask about stump grinding when you call for your free estimate.

Can you remove a fallen tree tonight?

Yes. Strong Roots Co. offers twenty-four-hour emergency tree service, seven days a week, across West Jordan, the Salt Lake Valley, and Park City. If a tree has fallen on your home, driveway, or yard, call (801) 330-1444 any time, day or night, and we'll respond as quickly as conditions safely allow.

Do you remove trees in Park City?

Yes. Along with our West Jordan home base and the Salt Lake Valley, Strong Roots Co. regularly removes trees in Park City and across Summit County — including dead pines, beetle-killed trees, and storm-damaged trees on mountain properties. Estimates are free in every service area. Call (801) 330-1444 to schedule yours.
