Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most — the same ones people ask Siri, Alexa, and Google.

How much does land clearing cost in Utah?

Land clearing is priced by the job — the size of the property, how dense the trees and brush are, and what access is like all change the price, so there's no flat rate. Strong Roots Co. gives free on-site estimates: call (801) 330-1444 and we'll walk the property with you and quote the whole job up front.

Do you clear lots for new construction?

Yes. Strong Roots Co. clears lots and prepares properties for construction across West Jordan, the Salt Lake Valley, and Park City — removing trees, brush, and stumps so builders get a clean, workable site. We can also clear for fences, additions, and outbuildings. Call (801) 330-1444 to schedule a free on-site estimate for your project.

Can you clear land around my Park City home for fire safety?

Yes. In Park City and Summit County, Strong Roots Co. does defensible-space thinning — removing beetle-killed pines, ladder fuels, and overcrowded growth around mountain homes to reduce wildfire risk and open up your property. We're based in West Jordan and work across Northern Utah. Estimates are free — call (801) 330-1444 to schedule a walk-through.

Do you also grind the stumps when you clear land?

Yes. Clearing that leaves stumps behind isn't finished, so Strong Roots Co. can grind the stumps below ground level as part of the same project, leaving the site level and ready for building, landscaping, or seeding. Both services are quoted together in one free estimate. Call (801) 330-1444 to talk about your property.

Which areas do you clear land in?

Strong Roots Co. clears land from our home base in West Jordan across the Salt Lake Valley, up in Park City and Summit County, and throughout Utah, Wasatch, Davis, and Weber counties — from single residential lots to larger parcels. If you're not sure whether we cover your property, call (801) 330-1444 and ask — estimates are free.
