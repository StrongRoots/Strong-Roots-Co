Mature-Tree Care for Holladay’s Established Neighborhoods

Holladay’s neighborhoods sit right up against the Wasatch foothills near the mouths of Big and Little Cottonwood Canyons, and the trees show it — some of the oldest, largest shade trees in the Salt Lake Valley grow here. Big cottonwoods, maples, oaks, and pines tower over established streets, and they need a different kind of care than the young trees in newer cities: careful crown work, deadwood removal, and honest advice about when a heritage tree can be saved with pruning and when it’s genuinely time for removal.

  • Mature cottonwood, maple, and oak removal in controlled sections
  • Crown thinning and deadwood removal for wind and snow resilience
  • Stump grinding with full cleanup, ready for replanting
  • 24/7 emergency response after canyon wind and snow storms

Canyon winds roll straight down into Holladay, and heavy canyon snow loads snap weak limbs every winter. We prune for wind and snow resilience — thinning crowns, removing deadwood, and raising canopies over driveways and walkways. When a tree has to come down, we take it in small controlled sections to protect the landscaping these older properties are known for, then grind the stump so you can replant. Every job finishes with a full cleanup, and every estimate is free and in person — call or text (801) 330-1444.
