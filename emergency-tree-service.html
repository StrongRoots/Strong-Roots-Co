A tree fell on my roof. What should I do first?

First, keep everyone — including pets — away from the tree and any downed lines, and don't climb up to inspect the damage. Then call Strong Roots Co. at (801) 330-1444, any time of day or night. Our emergency crew will remove the tree from the structure carefully and clear the immediate hazard so your home is safe.

Yes. Strong Roots Co. handles storm damage in Park City and Summit County, including trees downed by heavy snow loads and high winds around mountain homes.
