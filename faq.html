Trees, Timing & Tree Health

Can you work around power lines?

If a tree is touching main power lines, call your utility company first — they clear those. We handle everything else, including clearance around service drops to your home.

Can you save my sick or dying tree?

Sometimes — call us for an honest assessment. If pruning or treatment can save it, we'll tell you; if it can't be saved, we'll tell you that too.

When is the best time to prune trees?

Late fall through winter dormancy is ideal for most species. Dead, damaged, or dangerous limbs should come off any time of year.

Do you work in winter?

Yes, year-round. Winter is actually great for pruning and removals — dormant trees and firm ground mean cleaner work.

How long does a tree removal take?

Most residential removals are done in a day. Large or technical trees can take longer — your free estimate includes a timeline.
