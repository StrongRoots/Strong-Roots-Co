Mountain Tree Care, Done Right

Park City sits at 7,000 feet, and its trees live a harder life than valley trees — heavy snow loads, thin rocky soil, bark beetles, and wildfire risk. We bring full-size equipment and a valley crew's work ethic up the canyon regularly.

🌲

Pine & Beetle-Kill Removal

Dead and beetle-killed pines are a hazard and a fire risk. We safely fell and remove them — even on steep lots — and haul everything out.

🍂

Aspen & Ornamental Pruning

Aspens, maples, and ornamentals pruned for health and shape, with timing that suits the short mountain growing season.

⛈️

Storm & Snow-Load Damage

Split limbs, uprooted trees, and blocked driveways after big storms. Our 24/7 emergency line covers Park City and Summit County.

🔥

Defensible Space & Thinning

Lot thinning and ladder-fuel reduction around cabins and homes — smart fire mitigation that keeps your property insurable.

Stump Grinding
