Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most — the same ones people ask Siri, Alexa, and Google.

How much does stump grinding cost in the Salt Lake Valley?

Stump grinding is priced by the stump — mainly its diameter, the wood's hardness, and how easy it is to reach — so there's no flat rate. Strong Roots Co. gives free estimates: call (801) 330-1444, tell us about your stump, and we'll quote the exact price before any grinding starts, anywhere in the Salt Lake Valley.

Do you grind the stump below ground level?

Yes. Strong Roots Co. grinds stumps below ground level, not just flush with the surface. Grinding below grade means the stump won't trip anyone, wreck a mower blade, or poke back through your lawn, and the spot can be covered with soil and seed or sod right away. Call (801) 330-1444 for a free estimate.

Can I plant a new tree or lay sod where the stump was?

Yes. Once a stump is ground below grade and the area is leveled, it's ready for sod, garden beds, or new planting — that's the main reason most homeowners grind stumps instead of leaving them. If you're planning a replacement tree, Strong Roots Co. can talk through placement during your free estimate. Call (801) 330-1444.

Can you grind more than one stump in the same visit?

Yes. If you've had several trees removed — or inherited a yard full of old stumps — Strong Roots Co. can grind them all in one visit and quote them together in a single free estimate. Bundling stumps into one trip is usually the fastest way to get your whole yard back. Call (801) 330-1444 to schedule.

Do you grind stumps in Park City?

Yes. Strong Roots Co. grinds stumps in Park City and Summit County as well as at our home base in West Jordan and across the Salt Lake Valley, plus Utah, Davis, Wasatch, and Weber counties. Whether it's one stump from a beetle-killed pine or several from a clearing project, estimates are free — call (801) 330-1444.
